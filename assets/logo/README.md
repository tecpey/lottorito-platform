## Construction &amp; Logic

- **Concept:** Two interlocking, facing **L** glyphs representing bidirectional trust between the core Lottorito platform and independent partner operators.
- **Typography Rhythm:** The 45° diagonal cut on the mark's lower and upper limbs mirrors the crossbar terminations of the custom lowercase `t` letterforms.
- **White-Label Constraint:** White-label partners may configure custom UI themes, but technical provenance, ledger records, and core platform attribution retain the immutable Lottorito identity mark.
